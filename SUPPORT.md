The best way to understand how to configure the voice UI and industrial dictionary is through our official YouTube channel:

## 🐛 Bug Reports
If you found a real error in the STT pipeline or audio drivers, please open a **GitHub Issue** in this repository. 
*Please search existing issues before opening a new one.*

## ❌ What is NOT support?
- We do not provide support for non-local STT services (Google, AWS, etc.).
- Please do not use Issues for "How to" questions.
